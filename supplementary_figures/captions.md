# Supplementary figure captions for Additional file 2

**Supplementary Figure S1. Reaction-identity analysis.** Counts of test rows that overlap training or validation under exact reactant–reagent–product identity, reagent-independent identity, and product identity. Labels report counts over the 40000-row denominator; the exact-identity bar also reports 782/39995 parser-valid rows.

**Supplementary Figure S2. ACE threshold map for the ECFP screen.** Number of datasets whose matched cliff/non-cliff ECFP-RF RMSE ratio meets each threshold from 1.15 to 1.35. Endpoint-deduplicated and inverse-multiplicity weighting are algebraically identical; pair-equal weighting uses endpoint appearances. The vertical line marks the threshold of 1.25.
